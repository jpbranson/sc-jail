# Population

* [IML population](iml-population.md) - The IML population is the count of distinct permanent IDs whose release date is blank or after today's Memphis date.
* [XFER bookings](xfer-bookings.md) - The XFER measure is the count of distinct booking numbers in the XFER jail workbook, which has several charge rows per booking.
* [Arrivals and departures](arrivals-departures.md) - Arrivals and departures are IDs appearing or disappearing between adjacent successful slots no more than 20 minutes apart; gaps stay blank.
* [Population forecast](population-forecast.md) - The dashboard's seven-day forecast of each source's daily average population is the latest complete day's level shifted by the usual weekday difference, shown against what actually happened.
