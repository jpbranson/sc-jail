# Plan and weekly run

* [Further analysis plan](plan.md) - Staged plan to turn the archive into measures over time, from the booking panel to length of stay, money bond, court linkage, and trends.
* [Weekly analysis run](weekly-run.md) - Local weekly job that refreshes the archive mirror and rebuilds every analysis product into a dated folder, run by the SC-Jail-Weekly-Analysis task.

# Snapshots and panel

* [Population profile](population-profile.md) - Aggregate profile of who the IML roster lists as held on one day, with definitions and first results from September 23, 2026.
* [Booking panel](booking-panel.md) - Private one-row-per-booking history replayed from every committed IML roster and detail observation and validated against every archived population.
* [Trends](trends.md) - Lines up weekly profile summaries by roster date and shows 23 measures side by side with the change since the previous run.
* [Source reconciliation](source-reconciliation.md) - Compares each XFER jail workbook version with the nearest IML roster by exact booking number, including shared fields and bookings only one source lists.

# Staged analyses

* [Length of stay](length-of-stay.md) - Kaplan-Meier time from commitment to listed release for bookings first listed after collection began, grouped by the filled-in record page.
* [Money bond](money-bond.md) - Finds new bonds, reductions, increases, and recognizance entries from record-page changes and follows each to release; also describes low money bonds.
* [Court linkage](court-linkage.md) - Joins pending hearings by booking number and calendars and indictments by exact case number to measure match rates, court-date changes, and indictment timing.
* [Re-booking](rebooking.md) - Kaplan-Meier time from a listed release until the same IML permanent ID is booked again, for releases seen during collection.
